# Moves

Every strike, grapple, hold and counter of the 60 wrestlers, with the move names confirmed in the game. Names follow the move names of the Western version, WCW vs. the World, where it has the move, and were checked against filmed clips of every move; where the game's move had no good Western name it has a plain descriptive one. Wording draws on the fan-written WCW vs. the World move lists (DragonFly Moon and Donovan Keith). Wrestler names are the real names; the Western version uses parody names for some of them.

## How the moves are chosen *(verified in game)*

- Only **Up** and **Down** change a grapple. Left, Right and no direction all give the same move.
- **Tap** Circle (release quickly) or **hold** it. A **close tap** (opponent very near) and a **far tap** are different moves.
- **Strikes:** Cross, tapped quickly or held a little longer; near (the opponent is right in front of you) or far (a few steps away).
- A **hold** (Circle held longer) does the strong grapple. The **special** is the strong grapple at full spirit (the spirit flash), for about 30 seconds.
- From **behind** the opponent there is a separate set (tap and hold; Up or Down give the same move).
- **Ground holds:** with the opponent lying down and close, Circle starts a hold. Which one depends on whether you are standing near his head or his feet. Cross on a lying opponent is a stomp.
- **Top rope:** touch the ropes near a corner, then hold the diagonal toward the corner and press Cross to climb and jump at the opponent (a different move for a lying and a standing opponent). With the opponent outside the ring there are two dives: a spring off the ropes, and a running dive through them (Triangle and a direction to run, then hold Circle). Heavyweights cannot do the dives.
- **Running and situational moves:** Triangle and Circle together from a standstill is a move of its own; Cross while running is another. When the opponent runs at you, tapping or holding Circle face to face gives a counter-throw. If you are grabbed from behind with a tap, a face button pressed straight away (spirit of at least 36) breaks free and throws the grabber (listed as *Break free of a grab from behind*).
- **Reversals:** hold Square + Cross to open a short catch stance; if the opponent's Cross strike lands during it you counter it (one move against a tapped Cross strike, another against a held one). **Corner:** Irish-whip the opponent into a corner, where he is left dazed; a held Circle grab on him is a strong move of its own, and he can reverse it by pressing a face button right after the grab.
- **Other:** Cross on an opponent lying on the mat is a stomp, drop or splash. L1 jumps over an opponent who is running at you (no damage). L2 is each wrestler's taunt, which raises spirit by 5 (2 outside the ring).
- A **counter** is what happens when someone's close tap is answered: the grabbed wrestler presses a face button right as he is grabbed (spirit of at least 36 is needed, and the chance is his spirit in percent). The counter rows under a wrestler are the counters to *his* close tap; most just free the grabbed wrestler, a few roll the grabber up into a pin.
- † = a throw that ends with a pin count (the opponent can kick out). ‡ = a hold/submission (no count; the opponent gives up if low on spirit and health).

## What the star on the wrestler select screen means

Nothing in the game: it is only part of the picture. No data marks a star move, and the finisher the game actually has is the special (full spirit). Several wrestlers with different star names share one special.
