## File formats

Message blocks: `@ id`, source lines (`JP `), translation lines (`EN `); no `EN` lines = original
kept. A file's header says what it holds and its limits (`# limit lines=3 chars=28` is checked by
lint). Tables: original columns beside English ones (`japanese`/`english`, or `X`/`en_X`). See
`tools/blocks.py` and `tools/textdb.py`.

## Glossary

`translation/glossary.csv` (`japanese, english, notes`): lint warns when a source line contains a
term and the English doesn't use its translation (several accepted forms: `A | B`). Prefer
established community names (existing English guides) over new coinages.

## Decisions
- Translation style (decided 2026-11-04): keep wording close to the Japanese (e.g. 公式戦 = Official Match, 炎の七番勝負 = Seven Bouts of Flame); shorten only where the picture/box needs it, and then use WCW vs. the World's wording as the guide for what fit well.
- Scope (decided 2026-11-04): translate everything the player reads or needs to understand the game: all on-screen displays and menus. Kept as is: the staff credits, and basic game art that doesn't affect understanding (e.g. text on a wrestler's T-shirt). Wrestler names ship as two patch variants: real names and parody names. WCW vs. the World (the Western version) is the starter guideline for English wording.
